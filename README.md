# Healthcare-Analytics-Dashboard
Papollo Hospitals – Leads Flow Dashboard

• Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze ₹190.43M total billing amount and overall patient flow.

• Analyzed 7.2K+ bed occupancy across Private (3.6K), General (2.4K), and ICU (1.2K) categories.

• Tracked 4.83K+ patient feedback records per doctor to evaluate feedback volume and patient experience.

• Analyzed 7.25K+ patients across major diagnoses, including Viral Infection (2.00K), Flu (1.72K), Malaria (1.43K), Typhoid (1.15K), Pneumonia (0.67K), and Fracture (0.28K).

• Compared Billing Amount and Health Insurance Amount across 5 medical procedures — CT Scan, Ultrasound, MRI, Blood Test, and X-Ray.

• Added interactive Patient ID and Date Range filters to enable detailed analysis of individual patients and different time periods.

• Used Power BI, Excel, Data Cleaning, DAX, and Data Visualization to transform hospital data into actionable business insights.
